# Changelog

## 2026-11-18 · Matchday redesign

The profile now follows the stadium portfolio: a floodlit night stadium,
lime signage and every project as a player.

### Added
- Animated hero with floodlights, a self-drawing name, a rotating role line
  and a live match ticker.
- Numbered section banners and in-page navigation with back-to-top links.
- Big-screen scoreboard with rolling odometer digits.
- Captain's feature card for MIRAGE.
- The Starting XI on an animated 4-3-3 pitch, plus a linked card per project.
- The bench, the trophy cabinet, roles and education.
- Portfolio call-to-action and a full-time footer with a crowd wave.
- `scripts/` generator, `tests/` checks, a `Makefile` and a CI workflow that
  keeps the artwork in sync with its data.

### Changed
- Dark, stadium-styled toolkit badges and streak card.
- The 3D contribution graph uses the night theme.
- Links point to the stadium portfolio at stadium-portfolio.vercel.app.

### Removed
- The old light-theme hero, "what I do" panel and footer, with their script.

## Earlier

- Profile README with a 3D hero, data-science focus and a 3D contribution graph.
